Advanced Downtime

These downtime activies are only available to certain classes, and only to characters 2nd level and higher.

Crime

Only Thieves, Acrobats, Animalkin, Kobold and Warlocks can pursue crime.

Thieves have advantage on ability checks made for crime.

Heist

A heist is a single large crime or scheme. Get a note card to keep track of your progress on the heist.

Tilling a Vacancy

Scoping out a new heist takes one downtime.

  1. Write the location and the target on your heist card.
  2. Roll a number of d12 equal to your level. This is the value of the heist in sp.
  3. Roll a d6. This is the potential of the heist.
  4. Roll 2d4. This is the heat of the heist.
  5. Write each ability score.

Boredom

If you're up for a challenge, the heat of the heist becomes 10, and the value increases tenfold.

Risk

Whenever you take a risk, cross off an ability score from your hiest card. If none remain, take your pick instead. Check that ability. If you fail, there is an X-in-8 chance of trouble where x is the the heat of the heist.

After the first trouble, the heist loses two potential and gains one heat. Additionally, you must pay a fine equal to twice the value of the heist, and perform a downtime of service.

After the second trouble, you end the heist, pay a fine of four times the value, and must perform two downtimes of service.

Losing a Glove

If you are unable to pay the fine, you instead have a hand cut off. If you have already lost both hands, you are executed instead.

Actions

Each downtime, you can take one action on an existing heist.

Bite the Blow

If the heist has at least one potential, take a risk to get paid 20% of the value. Reduce the potential by one.

Fence

When a heist has zero potential, take a risk to end the heist, and get paid 100% the value.

Till a Rounder

Invite another character to the heist. Each downtime, including this one, they can use their downtime to take one action.

Pay the Prigs

Add an NPC accomplice’s name to the heist card. Each accomplice takes a 10% cut, but gives a -2 bonus on the ability check for risk.

Tide the Vacancy

Increase the potential by one.

Tide the Heat

Spend a downtime carousing. Reduce the heat by one, to a minimum of three.

Grease the Hamlet

Pay 1 gp. Take a risk to automatically succeed the next three risks.

Devotion

Only divine magic-users can preform devotion. The first time they do, they must describe the ritual they use for their devotion. If it is satisfactory, they will recieve a vision.

Then, the ritual can be repeated. Pay upto your level in gp. Certain events happen when you reach a total amount of gp devoted.

gp Event
2 +1 HP
3 Your bandages heal +2
5 Restore a deity disfavor
7 Bestow a blessing on a willing player character: They can memorize and cast one 1st level spell from your class of your choice, but most follow the tenets of your religion.
11 Recive a quest. No more devotion until it is complete.

Embezzlement

Only Clerics and Princesses can engage in embezzlement.

Vacancy

You're able to find 20 times your level cp to embezzle.

In addition, taxes, unclaimed inheritance, and similar transactions from previous sessions can be embezzled.

Laundry

Roll a d6 for each 100 cp.

  • 1: Loose the money, and risk getting caught (see below).
  • 2: Loose the money, but don’t take any risk.
  • 3-6: Safely sequester the money into your own account.

Risk

Check your intellegence.

  • Success Safely sequester 150 cp into your account.
  • Failure: Caught! Perform a downtime of service for each time you have ever been caught embezzling.

Forging

Only Dwarves can forge. Choose one option.

Honing

Costs 50 cp. A honed weapon deals an additional d4 damage. When rolling a 4 on that bonus, the honing fades.

Repair

Fragile weapons break over time. Restore it to its full damage die. Costs 10 cp for each level of degredation. If the item is magical, it instead costs 100 cp per. Repair always takes exactly one downtime, and it cannot be used until fully repaired.

hibernate

Only animalkin can rest this deeply.

Healing

Have a 2-in-6 chance to cure a minor disease or neutralize a minor poison.

Dreams

Gain one rumor from a dream. 1-in-10 chance to have to battle a monster in the dream dimension. Same rules as downtime questing.

HP: Lvd4, AC: 10 AV: 19, Dam: 2 Reward: 200 xp + a strange item.

Magical Research

Spell books

Only arcane magic-users can use spell books.

Adding to a spell book

Arcane magic-users can only add to their own book, and only spells on their spell list.

All spells must be identified with Read Magic, and takes one downtime per spell level.

Adding from a spell scroll costs 100 cp per spell level, and destroys the scroll. The success rate is 80 + your intelligence score out of 100.

Adding from a spellbook that isnt yours costs 300 cp per spell level The success rate is 50 + your intelligence score out of 100.

You can also learn a spell from a mentor, if you have one. They must be of a class and level that could learn the spell. The chance that your mentor knows the spell is equal to their level divided by 5 plus the twice the level of the spell, rounded down. In addition to the mentor’s wages, studies cost 100 cp and one downtime, per spell level.

Creating New Spells

The player describes in detail the spell they wish to create. The referee decides if the spell can be created.

The spell must be of a level the character can cast. Research takes two downtimes and 1000 cp per spell level.

Creating magic Items

Only magic-users of 9th level or higher can create magic items.

The player describes in detail the item that they wish. The Dungeon Master decides whether this is possible and, if so, what materials are required.

Divine spell casters may only create items that they are able to use themselves. Arcane spell casters may create any item except those that may only be used by divine spell casters.

Creating magic items requires rare components such as expensive gems or ingredients from rare monsters.

If duplicating the effects of a spell, construction costs one downtime and 500 cp per level of the mimicked spell, multiplied by the number of uses.

The Dungeon Master must use discretion for other items. As a general rule, items should cost from 100 to 1000gp and from 4 to 52 downtimes to complete.
