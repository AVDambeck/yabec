Downtime

Downtime is an optional system to roleplay the character's actions between adventures, and provides benefits to players.

Each adventurer sitting at the DM's table gets one downtime before the session starts. That downtime can be used for any returning character.

Advanced Downtime

Second level characters of certain classes can instead perform advanced downtime.

Baking

Help at the local bakery. Prevent the first 2 damage that would be dealt to you this session.

The baker also is a local gossip. If the baker does not already have one, make an NPC card for them. Each time you bake, they will provide you with one rumor. If you provide a rumor in return, gain 10 xp.

Enhancing Equipment

Lose any amount of XP up to the sum of all six ability scores, and pay that many cp. Add that much XP to an item. When that item has 333 XP, it becomes a +1 enhanced item. When it has 3,333, it becomes a +2 item. When it has 33,333 xp, it has a 3-in-6 chance to become +3, or to explode.

Community Service

Performing service at the temple has no direct benefit, but may be required as penalty for crimes.

Except for the first time, characters can hire a goon to do the service for them, first costing 100 cp, then costing 1% more, compounding.

Deity disfavor will not be forgiven if you hire a goon.

Healing

A stay in the temple of your alignment costs 100cp, or one downtime worth of service. It cures mundane illnesses, has a 5-in-6 chance of curing mundane toxins, and has a 2-in-6 chance of removing curses.

Gambling

Play a number of games of Dragon Ante equal to up to thrice your level.

You might also hear rumors. Make two CHA checks. For each you succeed, ask the Dungeon Master for a rumor.

Dragon Ante

The buy in for each game is 50cp. Roll a 2d10 and see the result.

If you are a thief, add your level to the roll. If you roll two ones, you still go to jail, and the fine is doubled.

Challenging the red dragon

Roll a d12. On a 1, lose your buy in. On a 2-11, you win 55 cp. On a 12, win 350 cp, or...

Choose to challenge Tiamat: Roll 6d6. Choose any number to reroll. Repeat this three more times. If you have six of a kind, or a 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6, you win 666cp.

Wild Wyvern

Once you win (or lose) 101 cp or more in a single downtime, you are invited to a Wild Wyvern table: an exclusive game for the best players. It is the same as Dragon Ante, with the following modifications:

  • Buy in, as well as the pay outs, and penalties are increased by a factor of 100.
  • instead of 2d10, use 4d6-1d12.
  • Whenever you roll two or more of a kind (except for five fives and four fours), you enter double dragon: wager an additional 500cp, reduce the number of games you'll play by the number of a kind. Then add that many d4s to your roll.
  • When you roll five fives, you will 55555cp. When you roll four fours, you loose 4444cp.

Mentoring

Finding a mentor

Training, employment, and some magical research requires a mentor. The target number is equal to:

  • 50
  • plus your charisma score
  • minus 10 times the level of the mentor you seek
  • plus however many additional sp you spend looking

Roll a d100. If it is less than or equal to the target number, you found a mentor!

Make an NPC card for them, and add their level.

Employment

You can work form them. You can only work for a mentor of a higher level than you.

Your mentor pays you a number of cp equal to:

  • your level
  • times their level
  • times 20
  • times your bonus (starts at 1)

After, make a CHA check. If you succeed, multiply your bonus by 1.2.

Training

You can only train under a mentor of the same class and a higher level than you. traing costs 10 cp, times the level of your mentor.

Spend upto 100 times your Lv in cp. Gain that much experience. If you spend 20% more, your henchmen also gain half that much experience.

Selling

Try to get a better buy on your treasure.

Each downtime, you can find buyers for treasure equal to 500 cp times your level. If a single object is more valuable than that, you must work for several downtimes to sell it; other players may assist by using their downtime as well.

When you sell the item, roll a d100 on the table, and add your charisma score.

d100 Sale
<20 Robbed! 0%
20-29 Low ball: 75%
30-39 Low ball: 90%
40-89 110%
90-100 140%
>100 Swindler! 200%

If you get a low ball, you can refuse to sell, and try again next downtime.

If you get robbed, ask your Dungeon Master for a lead on the thieves.

Taming

Mundane animals can be tamed by anyone. Druids, and Elves, can tame magical animals, and tame mundane animals twice as fast.

Each downtime, make two CHA, and two WIS checks. (Druids and Elves make four of each). Once you gain a number of successes equal to 4 plus the HD of the animal, they becomes tamed. Then you can begin training it.

Each of the following skills costs a certain amount of successes. Once trained, the animal will automatically succeed at tasks related to the skill.

Team-building

Costs 50 cp per hireling. Each participating hireling has a 2-in-6 chance of gaining 1 loyalty, to a maximum of 8.

Questing

More adventuring!

Choose a challenge to face. Each round, you’ll make a melee attack against it, and then it will make a melee attack against you. You can flee at anytime.

Multiple characters can team up. Each character gets to make an attack, and the challenge will make only one attack, choosing a target at random.

If you slay the challenge, you gain its reward.

Fighters and Knights take half damage.

Sewers

HP: 1d8, AC: 9 AV: 19, DMG: 1 Reward: 4d6 cp + 10 xp + a rumor.

Outlands

HP: 3d8, AC: 8 AV: 17, DMG: 1d4 Reward: 6d6 sp + 100 xp + a rumor.

Swamp

HP: 9d8, AC: 5 AV: 14, DMG: 2d6+2 Reward: 12d6 sp + 1000 xp

Den of a Lesser Dragon

HP: 10d8, AC: 0 AV: 8, DMG: All save Blast for 8d6 (recharge 5-6), or 2d6 Reward: 2000 gp + 5000 xp + remove a deity disfavor.

Research

Use your downtime to learn! Not to be confused with magical downtime

Choose an object to study, such as a log book, artifact, or monster corpse. Make a number of intellegence checks equal to your level. You gain research points equal to the number of successes. Each time you research, you can spend points to learn from the subject.

  • One point: a piece of background info.
  • Two points: a specific or unique fact.
  • Three points: ask a question and get a yes/no/maybe answer.
  • Five points: ask a question and get an answer 20 words or less.
  • Twenty points: work with the dungeon master to invent a fact.

Worship

In order to worship, you must visit a temple matching your alignment. Each time you worship, you gain merit equal to you wisdom score plus 1d20. When you die, the temple will contribute cp to your funeral equal to your merit. There is also a chance they will be able to revive you, equal to merit/2000

Additionally, divine spell-casters might be able to forgive deity disfavor. The chance is equal to LVL+WIS/100

Lastly, there is a 1-in-12 chance that your prayers will return a rumor.
