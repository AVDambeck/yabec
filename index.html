Coming from another game?

If you are familiar with B/X, OSE, BFRPG or other OSR games, you are already largely familiar with Yabec! The design goals of Yabec are to be modern, and simple. Here's some of the highlights:

  • Combat uses descending armor class and THAC0.
  • Compatible with B/X math, but adjustments to AC, THAC0, and damage make combat go faster.
  • Copper piece standard.
  • Health is calculated as damage up, not health down.
  • It's a roll under system. Checks, attacks, and saves are all roll under.
  • There are fuzzy "zones" instead of exact range measurements.
  • There are more classes, and some old-school classes are given extra abilities.
  • Players, rather than the dungeon master, roll monster attacks as a "dodge roll".
  • Hazards are on a die roll rather than a clock.

Get Involved!

Yabec is available on Github. If theres an issue or a change you'd like to see, bring it up. I'm happy to discuss any aspect of this system with you.
