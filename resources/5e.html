5e Introduction

At present, the most popular TTRPG is D&D 5.5e. As such, its nescesary to addess the differences of YABEC and the expectations many players bring to the table.

To sum it up: YABEC is not just a new set of mechanics, but also a different style of play. 5.5e lends itself towards a neo-trad style of play: roleplay as powerful heros, with detailed personalities and backstories. Conversely, the OSR style of play focuses on challange and exploration.

XP for Treasure

Advancement is linked primerally to collecting treasure. Not milestones, not story beats, not slautering enemies.

Character Death

Combat is not "balanced"; its war. Lethally dangerous enemies are on every level of the dungeon. Traps may impose instant death. Players have few hit points, and healing is limited. We get to know the characters if they survive.

Vancian Magic

Magic is rare and limited. There are no cantrips or free spells. Magic requires hours of study. Casters need to plan a head to carefully choose what spells to bring into the adventure.

Simple Characters

There are no feats or species options. Characters have class, equipment, and some scores.
