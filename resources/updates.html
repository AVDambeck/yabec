Change Log

What's changed since the last version of Yabec? The current print version is 3.2! Get involved with Yabec development on Github!

  • Added terrible scar to dismemberment. removed broken leg.
  • XP score for potions has been reduced to 100 xp.
  • Overhauled travel again! the point crawl rules are sill available here
  • New pdf rending engine uses Selenium which fixes many rendering issues. A single voulme manual is now also available.
  • Cure poison and cure disease spells have been merged
  • Animalkin's claws become magical at 5th level.
  • mapping section now favors the white board method as it is the most intuitive. The visual aids for the call method have been removed.
  • Optional xp from freeing slaves. other sources of xp also listed as optional, though there'll be no change in how I score.
  • added rolless variant combat
  • added constuction rules
    • items list. They care specialized for different tasks. The lackey can carry many items, the man-at-arms comes ready to fight, and the supplier will restock common adventuring equipment.
