Fighter

Requirements:None

Health die:d8

Armour:Any

Weapons:All

Languages:Alignment, Common

Prime requisite:STR

Maximum level:14

A fighter fights monsters and defend other characters. They are soldiers, warriors, barbarians, and other tough folk. They can also become warlords and build castles.

Fighter Skills

Choose one skill. Each time you level up, choose another skill.

  • Berserker: While damaged 50% or greater, -4 AV.
  • Duelist: Each time you miss, -1 AV until you hit or combat ends.
  • Tactician: +/-4 bonus to Stunts.
  • Tough: Dismember with advantage.
  • Reckless: Deal 1 damage to a non-blessed weapon to add 1d4 to its damage.

Advanced skills

Starting at 5th level, you can choose advanced skills instead.

  • Beef Cake: +4 item slots. +2 healing when bandaged.
  • Bounty Hunter: +3 damage with blunt weapons.
  • Captain: You can act as a ship captain.
  • Commander: +2 Starting Loyalty and Renown.
  • Dual-Wielding: When holding a weapon in each hand: +d6 damage.
  • Forge Master: You can do Forging. See Advanced Downtime.
  • Forked Branch:
  • you heal 1d6 when begining single combat.
  • Sweep Attack: Make one melee attack against many monsters: Make one roll, AV + number of monsters. Must be using a two-handed weapon.
  • Monstrous: HD becomes a d12. Become monstrous.
  • Multiattack: Make 2 attacks, or an attack + a stunt.
  • Negotiator: +1 to reaction rolls. When in combat after a reaction roll, enemies have a -1 moral. When in negotiation, NPCs begin with +1 patience.
  • Spell Sword: Learn a 1st level wizard spell. Inscribing a sword takes 1 week + 5000cp. Once per day, cast the spell while holding the sword. (swords included short swords, long swords, claymores, and zweihanders.)

Stronghold

Any time a fighter wishes (and has sufficient money), they can build a castle or stronghold and control the surrounding lands.

After 9th Level

A fighter is granted the title Baron. Their land is then known as a Barony.
