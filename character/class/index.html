Classes

A class is an adventuring profession. This page contains a full list of classes, split into Basic, Advanced, Demihuman, and Expert.

Basic

The four core classes of the dungeon game.

Cleric

A monastic traveler who can turn away the undead. Later learns healing and divination.

Fighter

A brave warrior trained in combat; the jack of all war trades.

Thief

A cunning scout with a variety of sneaky skills.

Wizard

A frail sage who casts spells. Weak at first, but eventually learns powerful magic.

Advanced

Additional and non-traditional classes with ability requirements and more complex powers.

Acrobat

A nimble performer with a multitude of tricks.

Bard

An enchanting minstral who sings.

Knight

An honorable soldier, sworn to a liege.

Princess

An intelligent noble who carries royal status.

Demihuman

Fantasy creatures.

Animalkin

A template class that covers any kind of animal.

Dwarf

A stout mountain dweller that values craftsmanship.

Elf

Fae demihumans that live hundreds of years.

Halfling

A homely creature that is taken on an adventure to destroy a ring.

Kobold

A pathetic dog.

Expert

2e-esque classes with abilities that require additional resource management.

Druid

A follower of the old gods who has strange powers.

Mystic

A wanderer with uncanny powers.

Warlock

A sinister magician who's made a deal.

Hollow

An engimatic warrior who performs great feats.
