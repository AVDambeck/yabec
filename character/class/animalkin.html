Animal Kin

Requirements: None (see below)

health die: d6

Armour: None (see below)

Weapons: None (see below)

Languages: Alignment, Common, Sylvan

Prime requisite: CON, STR

Maximum level: 10

There are hundreds of kinds of animalkin: tigerkin, ocelotkin, wolfkin, antkin, eaglekin, and many more. They are natural creatures, having more in common with wild beasts than the civilizations they often find themselves in.

Animal Kind

You are affected by magic as though you are a magical animal, in addition to being a humanoid.

Beginning at 5th level, your unarmed attacks count as magical, being able to damage ghosts and similar creatures.

Animal Traits

Choose animal traits fitting for the type of animal you are.

Each has a point cost, and many have prerequisites.

You start with 7 points at first level, and you gain 1 point each level after that.

  • Armored (1): You can wear leather, chain mail, barding, and spiked collars.
  • Archer (1): You can use thrown and fired weapons.
  • Aquine (1): You can breathe underwater and swim fast.
  • Berserker (9 CON, 4): When at or below half HP, -4 AV.
  • Blade Weapons (2): You can use non-blunt melee weapons.
  • Blunt Weapons (1): You can use blunt weapons.
  • Caratine (9 CON, 1): Your base AC is 5.
  • Hungry (1): Once per session, spend a combat round eating a ration to heal 1d6+1.
  • Infravision (9 WIS, 3): You can see in the dark.
  • Keen Hearing (9 WIS, 1): +1 to all eavesdropping checks.
  • Keen Smell (9 CON, 3): If a Kobold, +1 to door checks. You're able to locate creatures via scent.
  • Large Natural Weapon (9 STR, 4): Your body itself is a powerful weapon. You may attack with a d10, melee, slow weapon at any point.
  • Leap (9 DEX, 4): Can jump 15’ in one bound, and 10’ vertically. If used immediately before making a melee attack, -2 AV.
  • Monstrous (3): HD becomes a d12. Become monstrous.
  • Nature's Favor (9 CHA, 5): +1 healing from druids.
  • Nine Lives (7): Nine times, choose to roll a base score of 30 on dismemberment.
  • Pack Animal (1): +2 item slots.
  • Pack Hunter (9 CHA, 2): Once per turn, when an ally hits an enemy, your next attack against that enemy has advantage.
  • Small Natural Weapon (9 DEX, 2): Your body itself is a powerful weapon. You may attack with a d4, melee weapon at any point.
  • Hiding (9 DEX, 3): In woods or undergrowth, 90% chance of success. In dungeons, 2-in-6 chance of success. There must be some form of cover and must remain motionless and silent.
  • Speak With Animals (9 CHA, 3): Choose one type of animal. You may speak with that animal as though by a Speak With Animals spell.
  • Climb Sheer Surfaces (5): Climb sheer surfaces. Make a DEX check ever 100', and whenever you take damage.
  • Tough (9 CON, 2): Dismember with advantage.
  • Wings (13 STR, 8): You can fly.
  • Yapper (9 INT, 2): Learn a new language.
