Patron Language

A warlock knows the language of their patrons.

Evil Aura

Undead and fiends won’t attack a warlock until provoked.

Divine Magic

See Magic for full details.

Unholy symbol: must carry an unholy symbol to use magic. Begin the game with one. If lost, it is difficult to replace.

Deity disfavor: Warlock must be faithful to the tenets of their alignment, and their patron(s). Take three at random.

  • Blind: Blind beyond touch.
  • Bloodbound: Refusal to kill one type of monster.
  • Bloodlust: Irresistably compelled to kill one type of monster.
  • Cursesworn: Use of a cursed weapon.
  • Fire Aspect: Sanctity of the flame. Can only use a lit torch as a weapon (d8, melee)
  • Forked Branch: Sanctity of single combat.
  • Hear No Evil: Refusal to be enchanted.
  • Life Itself: Sanctity of death.
  • Mark: Inscription of names.
  • Megalomaniac: Lust for power.
  • Monstrous: Become monstrous.
  • Natural Nectar: No natural healing. Heal 1d6 by drinking 1 pint of fresh humanoid blood.
  • Pursuit: Sanctity of the hunt.
  • Rosary: Bearing of a cursed amulet.
  • See No Evil: Sacrifice an eye and three fingers.
  • Spared: Use only blunt weapons, instead of non-blunt.
  • Speak No Evil: Refuse to speak names of any god.
  • Strike True: Missing an attack causes the weapon to take 1 damage.
  • Tithe: 15% tithe. Libate the tithe into a fire or lake.
  • Trophy: Collect one trophy per session, such as an ear or finger.

Redemption

If a warlock acquires four or more disfavors, they become either a cleric or a fighter.

Divine Magic

Spell casting: A warlock may pray to receive spells. The power and number of spells available to a warlock are determined by the character’s level. Their spell list is found at the end of this section.

Blood Offerings

Whenever a warlock casts a spell, or has a spell cast on them, they take 1 damage.

If a warlock is holding a dagger, as an action they can take damage for a chance of the spell being remembered. The chance is equal to X-in-8 where X is the damage, divided by the level of the spell.

Blood Libations

Starting at 6th level, a warlock can deal up to half the damage from blood offerings to a touched prone creature.

Stronghold

Any time a warlock wishes (and has sufficient money), they can build a keep or abbey and control the surrounding lands. 1d6 apprentices of levels 1–3 will then arrive to study under the warlock.
