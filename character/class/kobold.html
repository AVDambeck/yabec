Kobold

Requirements:Chaotic alignment

health die:d4

Armour:Leather, Chain, Shield

Weapons:non-two-handed

Languages:Alignment, Kobold, one of the following: goblin, orcish, dark elvish.

Prime requisite:STR, CHA

Maximum level:8

Kobold are canid creatures that dwell in caves. They stand 3’-4’, but often walk on all fours.

Monstrous: Kobold are monstrous. See p5.

Pathetic

Lower three of your ability scores to 7.

Kobold Skills

Kobold have the following skills.

  • Keen Smell: Kobold have a +1 door checks. Kobolds can detect creatures via scent.
  • Infravision: Kobolds have infravision and can see in the dark.
  • Hiding: Kobold have the ability to avoid detection, apparently vanishing from sight: In woods or undergrowth, a kobold can hide with a 90% chance of success. In dungeons, a kobold can hide in normal lighting conditions with a 2-in-6 chance of success. There must be some form of cover (e.g. shadows) and the kobold must remain motionless and silent.
  • Nature’s Favor: Kobolds get +1 healing when receiving healing from a druid or warlock, including bandaging.
  • Speak with Dogs: Kobold are able to speak with dogs and other canine as though by a Speak with Animals spell.
  • Natural Weapon: Kobold can use their bite as a natural weapon (d4, melee)

Pack Leader

Starting at 2nd level, a kobold can draw other kobold to its side. When enemy kobolds fail a morale check, there is an X-in-8 chance they are willing to become your hirelings, where X is your level. Once per session, when you do this, you can attempt a charisma check to increase your renown by one, to a max of 8.

After reaching 5th level

A kobold can take over a cave as a dwelling. 3d6 kobold swear loyalty to you as their new chieftain.
