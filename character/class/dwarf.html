Dwarf

Requirements:CON 9

Health die:d8

Armour:All

Weapons:All

Languages:Alignment, Common, Dwarvish

Prime requisite:STR

Maximum level:12

Dwarves are stout, bearded demihumans, about 4’ tall and weighing about 150 pounds. They live underground and love fine craftsmanship, gold, hearty food, and drink. Dwarves are known for their stubbornness and practicality.

Detect Construction

When searching, dwarves also detect flaws in construction, sliding walls, sloping passages and other stone architectural features.

Patch-up

Once per session, prevent a fragile weapon from degrading.

Infravision

Dwarves can see in the dark.

Anchint Rivalry

Elves hate dwarves. Dwarves can not have elvish hirelings, and have disadvantage on any social checks made with elves.

Tough

Dwarves dismember with a advantage.

Berserker

While a dwarf has damage equal to or greater than 50% of their health, their AV is reduced by 4.

Speak with stones

At 7th level, a dwarf can cast Speak With Stones once per session.

After Reaching 9th Level

A dwarf has the option of creating an underground stronghold that will attract dwarves from far and wide. Dwarves usually live in clans, so dwarves of the character’s clan will be attracted to live under their roof. Dwarves from other clans will generally be friendly and clans may collaborate in times of war or disaster.

A dwarf ruler may only hire dwarven mercenaries. However specialists and hirelings of any race may be hired.
