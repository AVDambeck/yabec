Knight

Requirements:CON 9, DEX 9, Alignment of liege (see below)

Health die:d10

Armour:Chain, Plate, Suit, Shields

Weapons:All

Languages:Alignment, Common

Prime requisite:STR

Maximum level:14

Knights serve a noble house. At 1st and 2nd level, they are squires, not true knights. Upon 3rd level, they are knighted by their liege and gain the right to bear a coat of arms.

Alignment: A knight must have the same alignment as their liege.

Chivalric Code

Knights are bound by a code of honor. Breaking code revokes knighthood; they become a Fighter.

  • Knighted: Service of the knight’s liege.
  • Sword and Shield: Defense of all in their charge
  • Forked Branch: Sanctity of single combat.
  • Guts: Death over dishonor.

Knight Skills

  • Knights gain a -2 bonus to AV when mounted.
  • Knights gain a -2 bonus to AV when in single combat. They heal 1d6 when begining single combat.
  • Once per turn, a knight can take the damage of an attack directed at an touch ally. Reduce the damage by half.
  • When a knight is healed by your liege, they gain -2 AV until fatigued. (apply only once)
  • When a Knight allows an opponet to retreat or surrender, they heal 1.

Hospitality

A knight of 3rd level or higher can expect hospitality and aid from nobles and other knights of the same alignment or social affiliation. A knight is expected to extend such hospitality in kind.

Strength of Will

Knights are immune to fear effects. From 3rd level, companions within a short distance of the knight gain advantage on saving throws against fear effects, and loyalty checks.

Honor

Starting at 3rd level, a knight can acrue honor points. A knight gains one honor point the first time each they use one of their knight skills. They can have a maximum honor equal to their level.

Virtue

When a knight completes certain deeds, they can gain a virtue. They can use their virtue by spending honor points. They can only activate one virtue per turn.

Taming

A knight of 5th level or higher can train magical animals using downtime.
