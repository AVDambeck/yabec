Mystic

Requirements:WIS 13, INT 9

Health die:d6

Armour:Leather

Weapons:staff, sling, long-bow

Languages:Alignment, Common, Deep speech

Prime requisite:WIS, INT

Maximum level:12

Examples:Councilor Diana Troy, urSu

Mystics are a strange wizened folk who attune themselves to subtle energies. They receive their teachings through deep meditation, rumored come from entities unlike any seen in this world.

Martial Arts

Your unarmed attacks deal d4 melee damage

While holding a staff, you can leap a 10' gap. Immedietly after doing so, you have advantage on attacks with the staff.

Mantra

You begin the session with zero mantra. While not in combat, you can quietly repeat a magical phrase. Each turn you do this, you gain one mantra. You can have up to ten mantra at any given time.

Upon reaching 7th level, you gain two mantra each turn, to a maximum of twenty five.

Mystic Powers

When you learn a mystic power, choose a number, an roll a d6. If you do not roll the chosen number, learn one power at random, rerolling duplicates. Otherwise learn one of your choice, and learn another power (choosing a number and rolling again).

At first level, you learn two mystic powers. At each odd level after that, you learn one more.

  1. Block: Costs 1 mantra. You gain a -1 bonus to AC for each free hand you have. Last until fatigued.
  2. Break Repoise: Costs 2 mantra. When you hit an enemy, the target must also make a physical save or lose all actions this round.
  3. Breath Fire: While holding a lit torch and a flask of oil, you can create a burst of flame. This consumes the flask. It affects all creatures in front of you within a touch range, and deals 1d6 exploding damage. When it explodes there is a mishap, and you are also affected. Any affected creatures can attempt a physical saving throw to avoid half of the damage.
  4. Darkvision: Costs 1 mantra. Gain infravision for one turn.
  5. Divination: Costs 3 mantra. You cast Portent. Once per sesssion, instead cast Augury.
  6. Fein: Costs 3 mantra. For one turn you appear completely dead. Ends if you move voluntarially.
  7. Feline: Costs 3 mantra. You can fit through a gap as small as your head, have advantage on saves to resist fall damage, and have an AC of 7. Lasts until fatigued.
  8. Janitor: Costs 1 mantra. Open a door silently. This turn and next, you can not be surprised.
  9. Kung Fu: Costs 2 mantra. When you hit with a blunt weapon, you can make an unarmed attack.
  10. Parry: Costs 2 mantra. Change a hit against you to a miss. Additionally, you can make a melee attack against the attacker.
  11. Pressure Point: Costs 2 mantra. When you hit, cause the target to be surprised and proned.
  12. Psychic Blast: Costs 10 mantra. All other creatures within the same zone as you take a d8 of damage per your level. You pass out and are incapacitated for the rest of the combat.
  13. Realign: Costs 5 mantra. Cast Lesser Charm. The spell fails if the target is of the same alignment as you.
  14. Relocate: Costs 7 mantra. Walk or climb to an adjacent zone. To observers, this appears instant. Additionally you ignore mundane restrictions to movement such as mud or manicals.
  15. Share Lung: Costs 4 mantra. Cast Cure Wounds. If the target is not the same alignment as you, heal with disadvantage. Use this no more than three times per session.
  16. Tough: Costs 3 mantra. Gain advantage on dismembering.
  17. Telepathy: Costs 4 mantra. Hear the thoughts of one creature you can see, unless it succeeds a magic save. Lasts one turn. At 5th level and higher, once per session instead cast Telepathy, and can send thoughts to the concentrated creature.
  18. Third Eye: Costs 4 mantra. Cast Detect Magic. Limit once per session.
  19. Third Eye: Costs 1 mantra. Gain the ability to use any normal weapon. Lasts until fatigued.
  20. Void: Costs 4 mantra. Cure a mundane illneess or neutralize a mundane poison from yourself. Alternitively, transfer a mundane or magical poison or illness, or a curse to yourself.

After Reaching 9th Level

A mystic may establish or build a monastery.

Once a monastery is established, the mystic will attract followers (2d6 mystics of level 1–3, and 2d10 fighters of level 1-2).
