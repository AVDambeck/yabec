Mystic

Requirements:WIS 13, INT 9

Health die:d6

Armour:Leather

Weapons:staff, sling, long-bow

Languages:Alignment, Common, Deep speech

Prime requisite:WIS, INT

Maximum level:12

Mystics are a strange wizened folk who attune themselves to subtle energies. They receive their teachings through deep meditation, rumored come from entities unlike any seen in this world.

Martial Arts

Your unarmed attacks deal d4 melee damage

While holding a staff, you can leap a 10' gap. Immedietly after doing so, you have advantage on attacks with the staff.

Mantra

While not in combat, you can quietly repeat a magical phrase. Each turn you do this, you gain one mantra. You can have up to ten mantra at any given time.

Upon reaching 7th level, you gain two mantra each turn, to a maximum of twenty five.

Mystic Powers

At first level, you learn two mystic powers at random. At each odd level after that, you learn one more of your choice.

  1. Darkvision: Costs 1 mantra. Gain infravision for one turn.
  2. Divination: Costs 3 mantra. You cast Augury. Limit once per session.
  3. Feline: Costs 4 mantra. You can fit through a gap as small as your head, and have advantage on saves to resist fall damage. Lasts until fatigued.
  4. Kung Fu: Costs 2 mantra. When you hit with a staff, you can make an unarmed attack.
  5. Precognition: Costs 3 mantra. Perform a search instantly.
  6. Pressure Point: Costs 3 mantra. When you hit, cause the target to be surprised and proned.
  7. Psychic Blast: Costs 10 mantra. All other creatures within the same zone as you take a d8 of damage per your level. You pass out and are incapacitated for the rest of the combat.
  8. Realign: Costs 4 mantra. Cast Lesser Charm. The spell fails if the target is of the same alignment as you.
  9. Relocate: Costs 7 mantra. Walk to an adjacent zone. To observers, this appears instant. Additionally you move as though under Freedom of Movement.
  10. Share Lung: Costs 7 mantra. Cast Cure Wounds. If the target is the same alignment as you, heal with advantage. You may only do this X times per session, where X is your level.
  11. Telepathy: Costs 4 mantra. Hear the thoughts of one creature you can see, unless it succeeds a magic save. Lasts one turn. At 5th level and higher, once per session instead cast Telepathy, and can send thoughts to the concentrated creature.
  12. Third Eye: Costs 4 mantra. Cast Detect Magic. Limit once per session.

After Reaching 9th Level

A mystic may establish or build a monastery.

Once a monastery is established, the cleric will attract followers (2d6 mystics of level 1–3, and 2d10 fighters of level 1-2).
