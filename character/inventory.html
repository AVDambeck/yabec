Inventory

# summary The number of items you can carry is limited by your item slots. Most items take up one slot. Heavy items take up two slots. massive items take up three or more slots. you can fit upto five small items in one slot. Tiny items must be stored in sacks, which hold upto 250 tiny items. ## hands You can hold two items. a shield occupies one of your hands. a heavy weapon requires both hands. # definition one item slot is about five pounds, or one hands worth of item. the base number of item slots is equal to your con.
