Inventory

Slots

The number of items you can carry is limited by your item slots. The number of slots that you have is equal to your constitution score, plus ten.

Most items take up one slot, but some items are labled as taking up two, or more slots. You can fit upto five items labeled as "small" in one slot.

Coins, and gems can be stored in sacks. They are either carried on your person,

Ammo and other consumables ar often tracked as part of the weapon.

A person's body takes three slots. The rest of their items remain in their backpack, and can either be discraded, or added to your inventory.

Hands

You can hold up to two items at a time. A shield occupies one of your hands. Many weapons also require both hands.

Encumbered

You can become encumbered, and double your item slots. However, you move at half speed, and have disadvantage on checks, saves, skills, attacks, and dodges.

Rule of Thumb

One item slot is about 5 pounds, 1 foot long, 1 foot tall, and 1 foot wide. If an item exceedes any of those dimensions its likely to take more than one slot. If it is much less than that, it's likely to be small. A very small item can be tracked like coins, in a sack.
