Character Creation

Get a sheet of paper, or print out a character sheet, to record all of your character's details. Make sure to do so in pencil, as these details change over time. If your character sheet ever gets too messy, you can always copy the info to a fresh paper!

Profile

Choose a name, class, race (human unless your class says otherwise), and alignment for your character.


Note the creator's name (probably your name) and the date they were created.

Stats

Write your current level (1) and max level given by your class. Then write your current EXP (0) and the EXP required to reach the next level.


Roll 3d6, and multiply the result by 10. Write that you have that many copper pieces (cp).

Ability Scores

Your character has six ability scores:

  1. Strength (STR)
  2. Dexterity (DEX)
  3. Constitution (CON)
  4. Intelligence (INT)
  5. Wisdom (WIS)
  6. Charisma (CHR)

To determine them, roll 3d6 seven times, and choose six of the resulting values to assign to your ability scores.

If you would rather not roll the dice, assign them from one of the following lists of values:

  • 13, 13, 11, 11, 8, 8
  • 16, 13, 10, 8, 6, 5

Make sure you have the minimum stat requirement to use your class.

Prime Requisites

  • If you have at least 13 points in one of your prime requisites, note that you get a 10% EXP boost.
  • If you have at least 18 points in one of your prime requisites OR 13 points in two of your prime requisites, note that you have a 15% EXP boost.

Stats

Yabec has several stats associated with combat and exploration. Like your ability scores, most of them will improve as your character levels up.

  1. Hit Points (HP): Check your class's hit die. Give yourself an HP and max HP equal to that die (d8 = 8/8HP, etc.)
  2. Attack Value (AV): Give yourself your class's AV for your level (usually 19)
  3. Armor Class (AC): Give yourself 9AC (the base AC for an unarmored character)
  4. Armor: The types of armor you can use, and whether you can use a shield or not.
  5. Weapons: The types of weapons you can use.
  6. Saves: Note your physical (PHYS) and magical (MAGI) saving throws based on your class and level.
  7. Class stats: If your class has any extra stats (like a Wizard's spell slots or a Hollow's Endurance), note them here.

Minions

Yabec allows PCs to have a certain number of minions (both hirelings and henchmen). See Hirelings for more details.


For now, write that you have 4 Renown and 7 Loyalty.

Skills

Note the skills provided by your class.

Locked Skills

Optionally, note the skills you haven't unlocked yet but are planning to/are guaranteed to unlock in the coming levels.

Languages

Note the languages you can speak based on your class and alignment.

Inventory

Give yourself 10 inventory slots, and any starter items provided to you by your class.
