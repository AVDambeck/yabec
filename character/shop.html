Properties

Each item can have any number of properties, which affect how the item can be used.

  • Blunt: Can be used to incapacitate. Required for Clerics.
  • Fragile (-1, -2, -3...): After dealing the maximum damage, the weapon's damage die is reduced, from a d12 to a d10, to a d8, to a d6, to a d4, and then breaking. Forging can be used to repair this.
  • Fired: Makes ranged attacks. Consumes ammo.
  • Reach: Melee attacks at close range, and able to reach through allies.
  • Melee: Can be used to make melee attacks.
  • Small: Takes up 20% of an inventory slot. Can be mixed (for example, you can carry 2 sling stones, 2 rations, and a whistle in one inventory slot).
  • Heavy: Takes up two inventory slots, and requires two hands to use.
  • Slow: Use only every other turn. After making an attack, the next round, your next action is used either to reload, or handle the follow through of the weapon. There is one exception: you may drop the slow weapon, draw a Small weapon, and make an attack with it.
  • Splash: Damages many targets in an area.
  • Thrown: Throw the item to deal damage or otherwise trigger its effect. Can be quickly recovered after being thrown.
  • Enhanced (+1, +2, or +3): Grants bonus to AV and damage for weapons, AC for armor and shields, and healing amount for potions and bandages.
  • Damaged (-1, -2, or -3): Cancels out enhancement and penalties stats in the same manner.
