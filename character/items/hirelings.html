Hirelings

Common folk willing to go on adventures. When you hire a new hireling, note their starting loyalty, upkeep (measured in cp), and give them a name (see Hirelings). If not specified they have 1d8 HP, and enough slots to carry anything in their description, plus one.

Dwarf300

Can design simple machines, and mine tunnels. Has chain armor, and a battle axe. Instead of helping on an adventure, they can heal 1 damage from an item.

Medic200

Can bandage. Has a 13 INT. Has 2 item slots, a bandaging kit, and will buy a new ointment while in town. Unable to fight.

Lackey100

Has 10 item slots. Unable to fight.

Lockpick300

Can pick locks as a 1st level thief. Has 1 item slot and thieves tools. Unable to fight.

Man at Arms300

Trained in fighting. Comes with chain armor, and 5 spears.

Navigator500

A ship or party with a navigator will never become lost by mundane means.

Sailor150

Trained to run small ships. Amateur fighters. Comes with a club.

Supplier100

During upkeep, they will restock their inventory. They have 15 slots. They will stock: 3 ropes, mallet and spikes, a 10' pole, chalk, whistle, flint, 5 sacks, an antidote, and 5 torches. Unable to fight.

Torch Bearer50

Can hold a torch. Has 2 item slots. Restocks 5 torches and a flint during upkeep. Unable to fight.

Ruffian150

Ready to fight. Comes with leather armor, a club, and a sling.
