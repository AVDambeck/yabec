Hirelings

Common folk willing to go on adventures. When you hire a new hireling, note their starting loyalty, upkeep (the price), and give them a name (see Hirelings). If not specified they have 5 item slots.

Dwarf75

Can design simple machines, and mine tunnels. Has chain armor, and a battle axe. Instead of helping on an adventure, they can heal 1 damage from an item.

Medic75

Can bandage. Has a 13 INT. Has 2 item slots, and will buy 5 bandages and 5 ointment while in town. Unable to fight.

Lackey25

Has 10 item slots. Unable to fight.

Lockpick50

Can pick locks as a 1st level thief. Has 1 item slot and thieves tools. Unable to fight.

Man at Arms75

Trained in fighting. Comes with chain armor, and 5 spears.

Navigator150

A ship or party with a navigator will never become lost by mundane means.

Sailor15

Trained to run small ships. Amateur fighters. Comes with a club.

Supplier100

Once per session, they will restock their inventory. They have 10 slots. They will stock: 3 ropes, 5 iron spikes, a mallet, 5 bandages, a 10' pole, 5 sacks, an antidote, and 5 torches. Unable to fight.

Torch Bearer10

Can hold a torch. Has 2 item slots. Restocks 5 torches and a flint when in town. Unable to fight.

Ruffian50

Trained in fighting. Comes with leather armor, a club, a sling, and 5 sling stones.
