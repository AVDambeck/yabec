Weapons and Armor

All costs are in copper pieces (cp).

Properties

Each item can have any number of properties, which affect how the item can be used.

  • Small: Takes up 20% of an inventory slot. Can be mixed (for example, you can carry 2 sling stones, 2 rations, and a whistle in one inventory slot).
  • Enhanced (+1, +2, or +3): Grants bonus to AV and damage for weapons, AC for armor and shields, and healing amount for potions and bandages.
  • Damaged (-1, -2, or -3): Cancels out enhancement and penalizes stats in the same manner.
  • Two-Handed: Requires boths hands to use.
  • Rare: Rare items can't be purchaced. They must be sought out through adventure.

Weapon Properties

  • Blunt: Can be used to incapacitate. Required for some characters.
  • Fragile (d12, d10, d8...): After dealing the maximum damage, the weapon's damage die is reduced, from a d12 to a d10, to a d8, to a d6, to a d4, and then breaking. Forging can be used to repair this.
  • Fired: Makes ranged attacks. The weapon includes 10 pieces of ammo. After a fight, 1d6 pieces of ammo used during that fight can be recovered. When in town, buy new ammo as part of your upkeep.
  • Reach: Melee attacks at close range, and able to reach through allies.
  • Melee: Can be used to make melee attacks.
  • Slow: After making an attack, your next action must be standby.
  • Thrown: Throw the item to deal damage or otherwise trigger its effect. Does not imply melee.
