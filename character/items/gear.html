Gear

All costs are in copper pieces (cp).

Antidote50

Herbal remedy. Effective against most poisons.

When consumed, gain advantage on saves against poisons, until the next shift. When consumed, prevent existing poisons from killing.

Bandaging Kit25

Contains gauze, needle and thread, and other essentials used to treat injuries. The kit has five bandages in it. Restock on upkeep.

Battering Ram30

Can be used to break through locked doors and knock enemies over. Two-handed. Takes three item slots.

Bait10

Fragrant meat delicious to certain monsters. Small.

Bait, Specialized100

Bait that appeals strongly to a specific kind of monster. Small.

Specify to the DM which creature the bait is for, and take note of it.

Bag of Caltrops20

A bag of metal spikes that can be scattered around a zone. Makes crossing painful.

Can be cleaned up carefully and recollected in a turn if not destroyed.

If not crossed carefully (which takes two rounds of movement and leaves you vulnerable to ranged attacks), deals 1d4 damage.

Small, thrown.
