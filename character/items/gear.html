Gear

All costs are in copper pieces (cp).

Antidote20

Herbal remedy. Effective against most poisons.

Ammo1

Ammo for any ranged weapon. Small.

Bandage3

Used to treat injuries. Small.

Battering Ram30

Can be used to break through locked doors and knock enemies over. Heavy.

Bait3

Fragrant meat delicious to certain monsters. Small.

Specialized Bait30

Bait that appeals to a specific kind of monster. Small.

Specify to the DM which creature the bait is for, and take note of it.

Bag of Caltrops20

A bag of metal spikes that takes up a 10' diameter.

Can be cleaned up carefully and recollected in a turn if not destroyed.

If not crossed carefully (which takes two rounds of movement and leaves you vulnerable to ranged attacks), deals 1d4 damage.

Small, thrown.

Chalk1

Makes temporary marks. 10 uses. Small.

Chest30

Requires two people to move. Can hold ten items.

Holy Symbol25

Divine spellcasters are required to carry a symbol of their deity.

Mallet3

A hammer-like apparatus that can be used to anchor ropes or hold doors. Small.

Iron Spikes3

Iron spikes that can be driven in with a mallet. Small.

Jug5

Holds one gallon of fluid.

Flint7

Lights flammable material. Small.

Mirror30

A handheld mirror. Useful for looking around corners and redirecting gaze attacks.

Ointment5

When used with bandages, heals an additional +1HP. Small.

Pulley25

Mechanism which allows a rope to pass smoothly. Useful for a block and tackle system. Small.

Pole (10')1

2" thick, 10' long. Made of wood. Useful for probing.

Quiver20

Holds 20 additional pieces of ammunition. Refilled as part of upkeep.

Ration20

Large bundle of preserved food for long journeys.

Rope (50')5

Can hold the weight of three humans.

Sack1

Can hold 100 coins or gems.

Sling Stone0

Does not have to be bought, and takes no slots.

Thieves' Tools25

Leather pouch containing various tools, including: lockpicks, lever bar, shims, file, twine, narrow scissors, pliers, and wire.

Torches1

Provides light in a 10' radius until a burn hazard (see Dungeon Exploration). Small.

Whistle10

Makes a loud noise when blown. Small.

Miscellaneous Tool15

Common tools, such as pickaxes, crowbars and shovels. Ask your DM first.

Miscellaneous Wooden Object10

Common wooden objects, such as stools, tables, and chairs. Ask your DM first.

Miscellaneous Metal Object30

Common metal objects, such as pots, pans, and buckets. Ask your DM first.
