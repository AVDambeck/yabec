Specialists

Hirelings with advanced skillsets. The rates listed are a common price. Specialists must be sought out through roleplay before being hired.

Alchemist10,000

Can create potions based on a sample or recipe. See Magical Research. Can research new potions, but takes twice as long and costs twice as much.

Animal Trainer100

Can handle up to four mundane animals, or one magical animal.

Assistant50

A specialist can have up to two assistants. Assistants are half as effective.

Blacksmith150

Creates metal objects, including weapons and armor, at a rate of 100 cp per month.

Engineer1,000

Expert in planning and managing construction. Required to build strongholds. One Engineer needed per 1,000,000 cp of construction.

Journeyman Torch Bearer30

Can hold a torch. Has 3 item slots. Has leather armor and 9HP. Restocks 5 torches and a flint when in town. Unable to fight.

Magic User500

Can cast a level 1 wizard spell. Will flee from danger.

Ship Captain250

Seasoned sailor who can command a larger ship.

Soldier Captain1000

Has 3+1HD, and 15 AV. Comes with plate armor, a sword +1, a long bow, and a quiver. When a group of soldiers is lead by a captain, their morale and loyalty are increased by one.

Soldier1500

A skilled fighter. Has 3HD, and 15 AV. Comes with plate armor, a sword, a long bow, and a quiver.

Spy1000

Gathers information on a person or group. Chance of success is 100% for unsuspecting groups, 3-in-6 for disconcerning groups, and 1-in-6 for hostile groups.
