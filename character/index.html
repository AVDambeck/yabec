Classes adventuring proffessions.

Cleric A monastic traveler who can turn away the undead. Later learns healing and divination.

Fighter A brave warrior trained in combat; the jack of all war trades.

Thief A cunning scout with a variety of sneaky skills.

Wizard A frail sage who casts spells. Weak at first, but eventually learns powerful magic.

Acrobat A nimble performer with a multitude of tricks.

Bard An enchanting minstral who sings.

Knight An honorable soldier, sworn to a liege.

Princess An intelligent noble who carries royal status.

Animalkin A template class that covers any kind of animal.

Dwarf A stout mountain dweller that values craftsmanship.

Elf Fae demihumans that live hundreds of years.

Halfling A homely creature that is taken on an adventure to destroy a ring.

Kobold 2e-esque classes with abilities that require additional resource management.

Druid A follower of the old gods who has strange powers.

Mystic A wanderer with uncanny powers.

Warlock A sinister magician who's made a deal.
