Overworld Travel

Most sessions take place entierly in the dungeon. However, eventually resources at camp will dwindle. Or maybe, the dungeon has been completely cleared! Either way, the rest of the world must come into the scope.

Lodging

The default case is where the party is camping in the wilderness. In towns, lodging must be sought out and paid for.

Shelter: it's assumed the party has tents and standard camping equipment.

Food: One ration will feed ten people / animals.

Time: Advance the calendar one week.

Defences: The camp should have a guard rotation established. A well handled intrusion will not be too disruptive.

Rest: The previous requirement satisfied, everyone will heal all of their damage, and be able to memorize new spells next session. Otherwise, they do not heal, gain new spells, and become fatigued at the end of the first turn next session.

Drinking Water

In many areas, potable water is plentiful. However, in deserts, at sea, and other inhospitable area, 1 gallon of water is needed per day, or suffer 1d6 damage and become fatigued.

Travel Pace

Players can travel upto 24 miles in a single day. Players must consume rations (one per ten people) during the travel, or have their travel distance halved, and take 1d6 damage. Players who are encumbered have their travel distance halved. For each additional mile traveled at a forced march, they take 1 damage.

The dungeon master will check for wandering monsters once every six miles.

Entering Town

The dungeon master should describe the town, and the procedure for entering it (guards, residant reactions, etc). Then, the dungeon master should provide a list of spaces. Players can go to these common places, and meet NPCs, such as merchants, an inn keeps. They can attempt to procure adventuring equipment, and secure hirelings.

When the players are finished in town, they should head out once again to a dungeon.

Optional: Downtime

When the players are in town, the may remain for a time to pursue research, craftmanship, or other endeavures. The maximum number of weeks a the party can engage in downtime is equal to the mean average of the number of weeks that each character expored a dungeon, since the last downtime. Characers out of time

If the party travels without one a character, e.g. a player can't make the session, there are two options. The character can forgo any town activities to be retconed to the party's current location at the start of a session, or they can meet with the dungeon master prior to the main session in order to play through travel and town.
