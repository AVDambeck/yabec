Player Roles

Using roles makes play smoother and easier for everyone.

Taking on a role at the start of the session scores Lvl * 50 XP.

It's possible to split a role's responsibility between multiple people. For example, there can be a caller for exploration and a caller for combat, or someone who writes a session report keeping track of significant events and characters while another player keeps score and notes AC and AV of each encountered enemy.

If the main three roles are filled, duplicate roles (combat caller / exploration caller) or additional minor roles (quater master) can be added.

Mapper

Keep track of the layout of the dungeon. See Mapping.

Caller

Act as spokesman for the party. Get consensus on what the party would like to do, and communicate to the Dungeon Master. Additionally decide where the party will go this session, and ensure every player has what they need for the adventure (rations, antidotes, blessed weapons, etc). Can significantly speed up play.

Score Keeper

Keep score, noting enemies defeated, treasure recovered, and people freed. Additionally keep notes of significant names, monsters, and behavior.
