Battles

Each battle takes a full day. Each side has an objective:

  • Capture a location
  • Repell or slaughter the opposing side.
  • Another, as determined by the dungeon master.

Creatures form units of exactly 10 members. The total number of HD in the unit.

Initiative is handled the same as in combat. Each round, each unit can only move, and then attack.

Attacks

Units can not be dodged, nor do they make attack rolls. Instead, aproximates the number of hits, using the table below, or the equasion 10 - ( ( AV - AC ) / 2 ) rounded down.

AC - AV Hits
0 10
1-2 9
3-4 8
5-6 7
7-8 6
9-10 5
11-12 4
13-14 3
15-16 2
>=17 1

Units get +1 hit for each magic weapon being used, and each magic item the leader of the unit is using.

Units get half as many if they are using d4 weapons, or twice as many if using d10 or d12 weapons (slow still aplies).

Each hit removes one HD from their target.

Morale

The first time a unit takes damage, they make a moral check.

When the unit has had half of its HD removed, they must check moral. If they fail, in addition to the ussual consequences, half of the members (at random) are slain, and the leader must succeed a physical save or be slain.

Regrouping

Creatures who flee battle can are able to regroup the next day, and then fight again the day after that.
